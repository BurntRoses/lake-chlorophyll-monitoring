# Third-party data and geographic material

Original providers retain their rights. The MIT and CC BY 4.0 licenses in this
release apply to the authors' contributions. Source observations and their
reformatted values retain the requirements listed in [data_sources.csv](docs/data_sources.csv).

The public archive contains the processed inputs required to replay saved models,
all derived research tables, fitted models, results and figure source data. Obtain original bulk downloads from the cited providers and place them under
`data/raw/` to rebuild observations and features. The source registry identifies exact product-version information that
still needs to be recovered for some older downloads.

Credit LAWA (Land, Air, Water Aotearoa) and LAWA partner agencies, under CC BY 4.0.
The LAWA workbook license and attribution wording are in
`docs/lawa_notices.txt`. Credit Shahvaran et al. for the global
satellite lake-water-quality dataset, and retain its DOI and CC BY 4.0 license.
Credit ECMWF/Copernicus for ERA5 and NASA for CyAN. USGS-produced data and Natural
Earth layers are public-domain material; WQP observations can come from other
providers and retain their source identifiers.

Times New Roman is used in publication figures and must be supplied by the user
for rendering. No proprietary font files are included.
