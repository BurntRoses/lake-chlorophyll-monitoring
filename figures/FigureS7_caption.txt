Figure S7. Predictor-window configurations and event-definition sensitivity.

(a) Coverage of five configurations at 10% capacity. Numbers are coverage percentages; colour is the percentage-point difference from Single within each window’s event cohort. Same uses three same-window base predictions with different seeds; Multi uses 31–60, 1–14 and 15–30-day predictions. Primary optimises the primary window; joint adds auxiliary objectives. Brackets identify tree depth and auxiliary weight α: Same-joint uses depth 4 and α=0.5, Multi-joint depth 3 and α=0.1; all use 60 residual trees.

(b) Multi-joint minus each comparator for 31–60 days (black circles) and 1–60 days (green triangles). Bars are exploratory 95% intervals from 5,000 paired whole-lake bootstrap resamples with lists and quotas rebuilt.

(c) Coverage after rebuilding evaluation events at the Chl-a threshold and high-record gap labelled in each row, using fixed scores trained at 75 μg/L. n denotes evaluable events; the ochre border marks 75 μg/L and 60 days. XGB and EN denote XGBoost and Elastic Net.

图S7 预测窗口配置与事件定义敏感性。

（a）10%容量下五种配置的覆盖率。数字为覆盖率，颜色为各窗口事件队列内相对Single的百分点差。Same使用三个不同随机种子的同窗基础预测，Multi使用31—60、1—14、15—30天预测；Primary优化主窗目标，joint加入辅助目标。括号标明树深度及辅助权重α：Same-joint为深度4、α=0.5，Multi-joint为深度3、α=0.1；均使用60棵残差树。

（b）31—60天（黑圆）与1—60天（绿三角）中Multi-joint减各比较器的覆盖率差。误差条为5,000次整湖配对自助抽样、重建名单及名额后的探索性95%区间。

（c）保留75 μg/L训练分数，按行标注的Chl-a阈值和高值归并间隔重建评价事件后的覆盖率。n为可评价事件数；赭色边框标记75 μg/L、60天。XGB、EN为XGBoost、Elastic Net。
