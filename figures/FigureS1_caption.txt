Figure S1. Information availability, event construction and training support.

(a) Sampling dates (circles) and result-availability dates (squares) are separated by 7 days. Only available results enter decision-date inputs. Future outcomes define positive, negative or unknown labels; known high values sampled within the preceding 60 days identify active lakes.

(b) High records at days 10, 25 and 50 form one event; the 70-day gap starts a new event at day 120.

(c) Left: year-forward fitting with five lake folds during 2016–2020 and fixed-model evaluation during 2021–2025. Right: nested base-score fitting excludes both the outer validation fold and the scored lake’s fold; training records precede the scored year. Fold sizes are 582, 558, 544, 586 and 574 lakes.

(d) Label support among 573,461 training candidate records in 2013–2020. Bars follow the 1–14, 15–30 and 31–60-day windows, with 1,649, 1,993 and 3,283 positive records. The base predictors use 136,122 supervised records; unknown-label candidates remain in quota competition.

图S1 信息可用时间、事件构建与训练支持。

（a）采样日（圆形）与结果可用日（方形）相隔7天，决策输入仅使用已可用结果。未来结局对应阳性、阴性或未知标签；前60天内采样且已知的高值用于识别活动湖泊。

（b）第10、25、50天的高值归为同一事件，70天的间隔使第120天成为新事件起点。

（c）左：2016—2020年按年份推进、采用五折湖泊划分的拟合，以及2021—2025年固定模型评价。右：嵌套基础分数拟合同时排除外层验证折与被评分湖泊所在折，并使用早于被评分年份的记录。五折湖泊数依次为582、558、544、586和574。

（d）2013—2020年573,461条训练候选记录的标签支持。三条柱依次对应1—14、15—30、31—60天，阳性记录数合计为1,649、1,993、3,283。基础预测器使用136,122条有监督记录，未知标签候选仍参与名额竞争。
