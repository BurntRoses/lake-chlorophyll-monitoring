Every fortnight, 10% of eligible lakes are selected by score. An event is covered if its lake is selected on at least one eligible occasion 31–60 days before the first high record. In the retrospective 2021–2025 evaluation of 2,844 US lakes, Single-window covered 461/593 events (77.7%) and Multi-window covered 463/593 (78.1%). Multi-window uses auxiliary lead windows during shared-tree training; the 31–60-day output determines the monitoring list. Relative-target refitting rebuilds labels and threshold-dependent inputs in the source region before refitting. On the same 718 Chinese relative events during 2020–2022, Multi-window coverage rises from 62 to 349 events. Lake A/B traces and conceptual trees are schematic. Map lines delineate study areas and do not necessarily depict accepted national boundaries.
