Figure S4. Reallocation of a fixed monitoring budget among lakes and rounds.

All schedules use 36,631 slots across 2,844 US lakes and 130 rounds at 10% capacity. (a) Paired lake-selection frequencies under score-only (columns) and history-aware (rows) scheduling. Ochre borders identify 281 and 278 newly reached lakes for Single-window and Multi-window, respectively.

(b) Cumulative distinct lakes selected. Dashed and solid curves denote score-only and history-aware schedules throughout panels b–d.

(c) Empirical distributions of intervals between consecutive selections of the same lake. The 30-day reference marks the recovery scale in the scheduling rule. First selections are excluded.

(d) Slot-concentration curves after ranking all lakes by total selections within each method and schedule. The diagonal represents equal allocation. Event-coverage outcomes are shown in Figure 3.
