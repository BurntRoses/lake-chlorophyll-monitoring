Figure 3. Event coverage, capacity requirements and monitoring allocation in US lakes.

(a) Upper: coverage of 593 high-Chl-a events 31–60 days ahead at 1%–30% capacity. Symbols mark the first capacity reaching Multi-window’s 10% coverage (463/593, 78.08%). Lower: Multi-window minus Single-window coverage across five lead windows; event denominators are 679, 644, 593, 679 and 681 in displayed order.

(b) Multi-window minus Elastic Net, XGBoost and Single-window coverage at 10% capacity. Bars are 97.5% paired whole-lake bootstrap intervals from 5,000 resamples (Supplementary Methods S5).

(c) Coverage against distinct lakes selected using score-only (open symbols) and history-aware (filled symbols) schedules. Arrows link schedules for the same method. Each uses 36,631 slots across 130 rounds in 2021–2025. Both rankers reach 510/593 events (86.0%) after history-aware scheduling. pp denotes percentage points.

（a）上：1%—30%容量下，593个高Chl-a事件的31—60天提前覆盖率。符号表示各方法首次达到多窗10%容量覆盖水平（463/593，78.08%）的位置。下：五个提前期中多窗减单窗的覆盖率差，事件分母依图示顺序为679、644、593、679和681。

（c）原始分数调度（空心）与历史感知轮换调度（实心）下，覆盖率与不同入选湖泊数的关系；箭头连接同一方法。各方案在2021—2025年130轮中均使用36,631个名额。轮换后两种排序器均覆盖510/593个事件（86.0%）。pp为百分点。
