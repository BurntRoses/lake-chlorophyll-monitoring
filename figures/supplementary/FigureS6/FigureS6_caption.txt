Figure S6. Historical Chl-a, reference availability and relative-event coverage.
(a) Pre-2020 Chl-a percentiles (25th–99th) pooled across 1,406,007 observations from 3,515 source lakes and 172,755 observations from 435 Chinese lakes, on a logarithmic concentration axis. Event labels use separate lake-specific pre-2011 references.
(b) Relative-analysis candidates with and without ≥50 pre-2011 observations spanning ≥365 days. Candidates lacking these references remain eligible. In New Zealand, 19 of 166 candidates have references, and 13 contribute the 24 primary-window events.
(c) Cumulative Chinese 2020–2022 relative events by lake-specific pre-2011 Chl-a q90. Endpoints are 718 evaluable events, 62 covered after absolute-target training and 349 after relative-target training. Equal q90 values are grouped. Guides at 25 and 75 μg/L delimit strata with 485, 215 and 18 events (Table S12).
(d) Relative-trained Multi-window coverage of events versus the fraction of event-bearing lakes with at least one covered event. Open and filled symbols represent 31–60 and 1–60 days, with arrows connecting regional summaries. Window-specific event and lake denominators are in Table S15. The two New Zealand points coincide at 100%. All coverage results use 10% capacity.
