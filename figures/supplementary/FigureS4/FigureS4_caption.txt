Figure S4. Paired lake-selection distributions and monitoring-slot concentration.
(a, b) Selection counts for Single-window and Multi-window across all 2,844 candidate lakes over 130 rounds. Columns show score-only bands and rows history-aware bands: 0, 1–13, 14–26, 27–65 and 66–130 selections. Cells show lake counts, zeros are blank, and both matrices use the same logarithmic colour scale. Ochre outlines the 281 and 278 additional lakes selected by history-aware scheduling. All originally selected lakes remain in the cumulative selected set, and the maximum count after scheduling is 65 for both methods.
(c, d) Slot-concentration curves for score-only and history-aware schedules. Within each method, lakes are ordered by selection count and the curves show cumulative fractions of lakes and slots. The diagonal indicates equal allocation. All results use 10% capacity; coefficients were fixed during development (Supplementary Methods S3; Table S13).
