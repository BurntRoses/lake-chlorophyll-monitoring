Figure 4. Cross-region coverage under absolute and lake-relative targets.

All panels use 10% capacity and a 31–60-day lead. (a) Absolute events gained and lost relative to XGBoost, as percentages of each cohort. Event denominators are 337, 86, 29 and 77 for China 2020–2022, China 2023, Oceania 2020–2022 and New Zealand (NZ) 2023–2024. Absolute events use Chl-a ≥75 μg/L and high-record gaps ≤60 days.

(b) Relative-target minus absolute-target training effects on the same relative events within each region. Bars are 98.75% paired whole-lake bootstrap intervals from 5,000 resamples; denominators are 718, 168, 128 and 24.

(c) Minimum rank/quota across eligible opportunities for 718 Chinese relative events in 258 lakes under the two training targets; values ≤1 indicate coverage. Quadrants show 314 gained, 27 lost, 35 retained and 342 uncovered events. Marginal histograms show priority distributions.

(d) Change in within-lake event coverage against pre-2011 Chl-a q90 (90th percentile). Symbol area represents event count; green, grey and red indicate increased, unchanged and decreased coverage. Relative references require ≥50 observations spanning ≥365 days before 2011. pp denotes percentage points.
