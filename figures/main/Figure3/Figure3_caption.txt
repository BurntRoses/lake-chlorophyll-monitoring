Figure 3. Capacity response, paired coverage differences and monitoring allocation.
(a) Upper: coverage of 593 US primary-window events at capacities from 1% to 30% in 0.25-percentage-point increments. The horizontal reference marks Multi-window at 10% capacity (463/593, 78.08%); symbols mark the first evaluated capacity at which each method reaches that coverage. Lower: Multi-window minus Single-window coverage across five lead windows, whose event denominators are 679, 644, 593, 679 and 681 in the displayed order. Ochre marks 10% capacity. Both layers use score-only lists; pp denotes percentage points.
(b) Multi-window minus Elastic Net, XGBoost and Single-window coverage at 10% capacity in the 31–60-day window. Bars show 97.5% paired whole-lake bootstrap intervals from 5,000 resamples, adjusted within the comparison families defined in Supplementary Methods S5.
(c) Event coverage against the cumulative number of distinct lakes selected under score-only (open symbols) and history-aware (filled symbols) schedules. Arrows connect schedules for the same method. All schedules use 36,631 slots over 130 rounds among 2,844 lakes. Scheduling parameters were fixed during development; both capacity-based rankers reach 510/593 events (86.0%). Figure S4 shows allocation distributions and slot concentration. Results are retrospective for 2021–2025. Absolute events use Chl-a ≥75 μg/L and consecutive high-record gaps ≤60 days.
