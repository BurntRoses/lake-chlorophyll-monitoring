Figure 1. Study lakes, monitoring workload and event-level evaluation.
(a) Locations of 2,844 US study lakes in the continental-US Albers equal-area projection (EPSG:5070). Dark red triangles mark the 354 lakes with 593 evaluable primary-window events; open blue circles mark the other 2,490 lakes. Dashed rectangles and solid leaders locate the Upper Midwest (A; 1,430 lakes) and Florida (B; 599 lakes) enlargements. USGS 3DEP terrain uses a common 0–4,000 m elevation scale, and geographic outlines are from Natural Earth. Scale bars show 1,000 km nationally and 200 km in each inset, calibrated to ellipsoidal geodesic distances at local reference positions. The arrow indicates local true north.
(b) Observed sampling fractions across 78 fortnightly rounds in 2016–2018. Sampling occurred in 20,027 of 218,838 eligible lake-periods, giving a pooled fraction of 9.15%. The dashed line marks the 10% quota used in the evaluation scenario.
(c) Fortnightly top-k selection, where k is 10% of eligible lakes, rounded up. Selections 59 and 45 days before the first high record cover one event in the 31–60-day window. High records have chlorophyll a (Chl-a) ≥75 μg/L; consecutive high records within a lake separated by ≤60 days form one event. Each event counts once per window. Models were developed using 2011–2020 records and evaluated retrospectively during 2021–2025. Geographic sources: USGS 3DEP (https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer) and Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/).
The schematic in panel c was drawn with Python/SVG code assisted by OpenAI Codex (OpenAI).
Map lines delineate study areas and do not necessarily depict accepted national boundaries.
